═══ 2. DRIVER REGISTRATION ═══
Route /drivers/apply — public form, one page, grouped sections, all labels sentence case.
Fields: full name, email, phone, home island (select), years operating a boat (number),
boat classes qualified for (multi-checkbox: water taxi, cruiser, catamaran, speedboat,
rescue), license/certification ID, languages (multi), typical availability (weekday/weekend/
overnight), and three short-answer textareas (min 100 chars each, live counter):
  "Describe your experience on these waters."
  "Describe a difficult situation on the water and how you handled it."
  "What emergency or first-aid training do you have?"
Consent checkbox: "I confirm this information is accurate."

POST /api/drivers/applications → { id, status: 'pending', submittedAt }. Zod-validate; return
{ error, code, field } and highlight the offending field inline. Disable submit while in
flight. On success show a confirmation panel with the application ID and "We'll review your
application and email you." — do not clear the page to blank.
Add GET /api/drivers/applications + a simple /drivers/applications review list (pending /
approved / rejected) with approve+reject actions; approving creates a Driver record.

═══ 3. DRIVER REVIEWS & RATINGS ═══
New route /drivers/:id — driver profile: name, avatar initials, home island, years active,
trips completed, boat classes, languages.
Rating summary: large average to 1dp, filled star row, total review count, and a 5→1 bar
distribution.
Reviews list below: reviewer name, star row, relative date, body text. Newest first,
paginated 10 at a time. Empty state: "No reviews yet."

Write-review form (signed-in users only; if the trip history supports it, require a
completed trip with that driver and show "You can review a driver after your trip"):
  POST /api/drivers/:id/reviews  { rating 1-5, body ≤500 chars }
  GET  /api/drivers/:id/reviews
  One review per user per driver — return 409 ALREADY_REVIEWED with an edit option.
Recompute and persist driver.rating as the live mean of reviews (seeded ratings become the
starting reviews so the number never contradicts the list). Update it everywhere the rating
appears — fleet cards, trip page, boat details.

Star input must be a real radio group: keyboard-operable (arrows + enter), aria-label
"Rate N out of 5", visible focus ring, 44px targets. Not clickable divs.

Link driver names to /drivers/:id from fleet cards and the trip page.
Seed 3-6 reviews for ~10 drivers so the feature isn't empty in a demo.
